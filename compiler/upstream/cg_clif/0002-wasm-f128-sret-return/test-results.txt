## What was not run, and why

- **Plain upstream cg_clif build with nightly-2026-11-17**: not run. Upstream
  `rust-toolchain.toml` now requests nightly-2026-11-17, which is not in the
  isolated rustup; installing it and rebuilding the 0.137 Cranelift dependency
  tree was outside the disk/time budget. The semantic test above runs the same
  source on nightly-2026-10-19, which is what the pinned tree uses.
- **`TARGET_TUPLE=wasm32-unknown-unknown` suite run**: not run; only
  `wasm32-wasip1` was used, matching the stage-C sysroot target. Both targets
  were exercised by the direct compile checks.
- **Native suite**: not run. The change is gated on `Arch::Wasm32`/
  `Arch::Wasm64`; native `indirect_f128` is unchanged. The wasm target suite is
  the relevant one.
- **LLVM/Clang ABI comparison**: not run; no wasm-capable clang in the
  isolated toolchain.
