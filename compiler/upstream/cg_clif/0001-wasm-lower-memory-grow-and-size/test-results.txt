## What was not run, and why

- **Plain upstream cg_clif `cargo check`**: impossible, not merely skipped.
  Upstream `Cargo.toml` has no `clif-to-wasm` dependency, so
  `clif_to_wasm::WasmIntrinsic` is unresolved. Upstream also has no wasm
  backend. This is why the test is stacked on the stage-A scratch tree.
- **nightly-2026-11-17 build**: not run; that toolchain is not installed in the
  isolated rustup and the semantic test runs the same source on the pinned
  nightly-2026-10-19.
- **Native suite**: not run. The added arms are only reachable for wasm
  intrinsics; native codegen is untouched.
- **`wasm32-unknown-unknown` suite run**: not run; direct compile checks
  covered that target. The suite used `wasm32-wasip1` because that is the
  stage-C sysroot target.
